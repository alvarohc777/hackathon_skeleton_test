# Design principles

- Plain language; short sentences; icons always with a label.
- Large tap targets (at least 44 px); works on small, older phones.
- Every amount in CHF, Swiss formatting (CHF 1'820.00).
- Several languages from the start; no text baked into images.
- The AI's suggestions always show what will change and wait for "Yes".
