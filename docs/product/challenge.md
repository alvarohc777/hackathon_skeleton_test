# Challenge: Beyond the List (PostFinance)

**Ask:** go beyond the plain list of bank transactions and turn it into insight.

**Our angle (draft, to confirm in the brainstorm):** migrants, refugees and people on low incomes in Switzerland. They get a monthly report in plain words, a dashboard they reshape by asking, savings tips with cheaper alternatives, alerts on suspicious payments, and PostFinance investment ideas, through an AI agent that explains and always asks before acting.

**Data we have:** sample CSV exports for two accounts (2025). Format and known problems: `docs/domain/data-format.md`.

**To fill:** judging criteria, deadline, demo length, submission format.
